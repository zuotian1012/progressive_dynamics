Flag L3 and finer: recover unresolved coarse backtracking at the current iterate.

This directory was copied from flag_l2_repair. README.txt describes that baseline.
Only advance_ipc.cu and psq_preview_stop.inl change its algorithm here.

UIPC_PSQ_UNRESOLVED_RESCUE=1 enables the extension on coarse levels only:
1. Ordinary line search still evaluates at most 8 trials with the existing caps.
2. Positive, resolved, consistent three-scale probes still allow a coarse stop.
3. Otherwise restore x_k, verify its full energy, and recompute the direction
   using the complete PSQ offset Jacobian gradient. No rejected trial is retained.
4. The retry uses the ordinary feasibility / CCD / energy checks and 8 trials.
   Even when its increment meets the Newton tolerance, E_trial <= E_current is
   required. A failed retry restores x_k and throws; it cannot become a preview.
5. After a successful retry, ordinary frozen-offset iterations resume. Repeated
   rescues are allowed, but every retry consumes the existing Newton budget.

The L0 first-iteration non-descent rescue remains. Its bookkeeping is generalized
from a fixed iteration 1 to a recorded retry iteration. Gradient/Hessian kernels
are unchanged from the independently checked L0 exact-gradient implementation.
This is an explicit repair extension, not an unmodified paper algorithm.

The initial L3 attempt exposed unhandled internal line-search failures concealed
by a later verified preview in the same frame. Old L0/L1/L2 logs contain 25/143/46
such warnings. Existing results are preserved, but the final continuation is
rebuilt from L0 with the new DLL and per-iteration acceptance auditing.

Build: scripts/build_flag_l3_fix.py --variant rescue
Run: scripts/continue_flag_strict.py --output <new-directory>
Audit: scripts/audit_flag_fine.py, plus existing geometry/non-descent/search audits.
No shared build/install, shared source, shirt runtime or shirt result is changed.
