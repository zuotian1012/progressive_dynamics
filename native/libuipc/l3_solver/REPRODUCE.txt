This starts from rest and uses completed L2 motion as the VelPro input.

Numerical behavior:
  1. Compensate binary64 matrix-vector products and the p^T A p reduction.
  2. Recompute the active operator's residual before declaring convergence.
     If needed, restart PCG from its current iterate, with the original
     tolerance and total iteration cap. At most eight reliable restarts.
  3. Start each Newton linear solve with tau=0. On diagnosed PCG failure,
     retry A_eff = A + tau*diag(A) with tau=1e-18, 1.6e-17, 2.56e-16,
     4.096e-15, 6.5536e-14. Fail if these bounded retries cannot converge.
     The diagonal term is evaluated separately in compensated arithmetic.
     MAS factors remain those of the original assembled matrix.
  4. Retain original energy line search and strict finest-level acceptance.

Nonzero tau changes the Newton linear approximation. It does not change
the physical energy, right-hand side, PD P/S/Q, mesh, material, contact,
friction, gripper targets, or time step. This is not a claim of an unchanged
linear equation. The residual check is for A_eff, including nonzero tau.
The frozen candidate's comment "ORIGINAL residual" refers to explicit
recomputation rather than the recursive PCG residual; it must not be read
as certification of the unshifted A when tau is nonzero.
